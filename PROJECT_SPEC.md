Create a professional, modern mobile application focused exclusively on **Speech-Language Pathology (SLP) freelancing and online speech and language therapy**.

The app should connect **children/patients and their parents or caregivers with qualified Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs)** for online therapy, while also helping users discover nearby SLP clinics for face-to-face consultation.

The app should have a clean, trustworthy, child-friendly but professional design. Use a simple interface that parents can easily navigate. The overall appearance should feel similar to a combination of a healthcare platform, professional freelancer marketplace, and child-development tracking application.

## 1. USER TYPES

Create three main user types:

### A. Parent/Patient

Parents or caregivers can:

* Create an account.
* Create and manage their child's profile.
* Search for SLPs.
* View detailed SLP profiles.
* Filter SLPs based on specialization, experience, language, consultation fee, availability, and online/offline services.
* Book online therapy sessions.
* Attend therapy through an integrated video consultation system.
* View therapy session history.
* View the child's progress and improvement over time.
* Communicate with the SLP through secure messaging.
* View therapy recommendations and home-practice activities.
* Find nearby SLP clinics for direct consultation.
* Give ratings and reviews after completed sessions.

### B. Speech-Language Pathologist

SLPs can:

* Create a professional profile.
* Add qualifications, degrees, certifications, registration details, experience, specializations, languages spoken, and areas of clinical interest.
* Add a professional profile photograph.
* Set consultation fees.
* Set online therapy availability.
* Manage their calendar.
* Accept or reject therapy requests.
* Conduct video therapy sessions.
* Maintain individual child/client records.
* Add clinical notes after every session.
* Record therapy goals.
* Track progress.
* Upload relevant therapy materials or assignments.
* Provide home-practice activities.
* Update the child's progress after every session.
* Communicate with parents securely.
* View upcoming and previous appointments.
* Receive earnings from completed sessions.
* View earnings and transaction history.

### C. Admin

Create an admin dashboard where administrators can:

* Verify SLP profiles and professional credentials.
* Manage users.
* Manage SLP listings.
* Manage clinics.
* Monitor appointments.
* Handle reports and complaints.
* Manage reviews.
* Monitor payments and transactions.
* Manage inappropriate or fraudulent profiles.
* View platform analytics.

# 7. CHILD PROGRESS TRACKING

Create a dedicated **Child Progress Dashboard**.

Parents should be able to see their child's improvement over time in a simple visual format.

Track areas such as:

* Speech sound accuracy
* Vocabulary
* Receptive language
* Expressive language
* Fluency
* Communication skills
* Social communication
* Voice
* Feeding/swallowing goals where applicable
* Other individualized therapy goals

Display progress using:

* Graphs
* Progress bars
* Milestones
* Session-by-session records
* Goal completion percentages

Example:

Goal: /s/ sound production

Baseline: 30%

Current: 65%

Target: 80%

Progress: +35%

The app should clearly state that progress scores are **clinical tracking information entered by the SLP and are not an automatic diagnosis or guarantee of improvement**.

# 19. PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Because the platform handles children's healthcare-related information, prioritize privacy and security.

Implement:

* Secure authentication
* Role-based access
* Encrypted communication
* Secure storage
* Consent management
* Secure video sessions
* Restricted access to child records
* Data deletion/account deletion process
* Audit logs for clinical records
* Privacy policy
* Terms of service

Do not allow an SLP to access a child's records unless the parent/caregiver has authorized the relationship.
